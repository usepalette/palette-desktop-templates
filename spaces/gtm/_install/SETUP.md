# Setup

**While this file exists, the GTM space is still being set up.** This page says how to set it up and when to delete this file. It keeps no progress of its own: the pages are the progress. With the person, talk about setting up the GTM space, never about this file.

This is the GTM space's setup only. A `SETUP.md` at the root of the workspace is the company's setup: separate from this one, and not finished by it. Both use the company's voice and messaging pages, so a page filled in here is filled in for both.

## Before the steps

The person needs a profile in the root [`people/`](../../people/README.md); the root rules make one if they have none. Then ask once for what they have, unless they already said: how the team sells today (founder-led, inbound, outbound or through the product), the channels it uses, its sales deck or pitch, and the GTM material it already has written down, such as a positioning doc, a strategy, a launch checklist or past launch posts. Whatever they have is enough to start: a sales deck alone is fine.

## The steps

1. **Fill in the company pages GTM work reads.** For each of [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../company/brand/voice.md), [`company/brand/messaging.md`](../../company/brand/messaging.md) with its Positioning section, and [`company/market/ideal-customer.md`](../../company/market/ideal-customer.md) that still says **Not filled in yet**, offer to fill it in from the material and the answers, following that page's own box, and write after a yes. A page that is already filled in, or says **Not confirmed yet**, stays as it is. If the material describes a product or names a competitor the workspace doesn't have yet, offer to add it the same way, with the steps in [`company/products/`](../../company/products/README.md) or [`company/market/competitors/`](../../company/market/competitors/README.md).
2. **Write the strategy.** Fill in [`foundations/gtm-strategy.md`](foundations/gtm-strategy.md) from the material and the answers, following its box: the strategy in one line, the current phase, how the team sells today and its channels. The phases and the bets stay in brackets until someone states them.
3. **Write the sales story.** Fill in [`foundations/sales-narrative.md`](foundations/sales-narrative.md) from the sales deck, the call notes or how the person pitches, following its box. Use the customers' own words for the problem wherever the material has them.
4. **Sort the rest of the material.** Show the person where each remaining piece would go, per [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) → Bringing GTM material in, and write after their yes. A team with no material skips this step.

Show the pages you wrote and ask the person to read them. A page they agree with loses its "Not confirmed yet" box.

## Not part of setup

The playbooks in [`playbooks/`](playbooks/README.md) are a starter point of view, and the formats in [`templates/launch/`](templates/launch/README.md) are usable as they stand: both are changed as the team works. Research arrives with the work. Teammates are added to the root `people/` when someone asks ("add a colleague"), and anyone who posts under their own name fills in their `people/<name>/tone-of-voice.md`. Customers get a folder once there is real engagement ("we just signed a new customer"). None of this needs to be done before the space is used.

## When to delete this file

When the four steps are done, or when someone says the GTM space is set up, delete this file and add one line to this space's [`log.md`](log.md) saying that setup ended and what was left open. "I'll do the rest another day" means setup goes on: leave the file where it is.

If the person came to do something else, do that first. Then say in one line where setup stands and offer the next step.
