# Foundations — [Space]

What this space **knows**: the reference material to load before working here, not the work itself. Stable, rarely changing, and the first thing to read before producing anything in this space.

## What belongs here

A go-to-market space keeps its strategy, its channels and how it prices. A product space keeps its strategy and what it has decided. A design space keeps its principles and its system. An engineering space keeps its architecture, its stack and its standards.

**Rule of thumb:** if it is stable and you would load it before starting a task, it belongs here. If it is a way of thinking, it is a [playbook](../playbooks/README.md); if it has steps, it is a [process](../processes/README.md); if it is work with a start and an end, it is a [project](../../../projects/README.md).

**This space's, or everyone's?** What the whole company needs (the voice, the messaging, how a product is described, who the company sells to) is not one space's foundation. It lives in [`company/`](../../../company/README.md), and this space links to it.
