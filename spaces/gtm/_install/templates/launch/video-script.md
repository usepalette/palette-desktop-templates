# Video script

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team scripts its videos, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

The script for an explainer or a launch video: the transformation in 30 to 90 seconds. Tier 3 only.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief; how the company sounds in [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../../../company/brand/voice.md), and how it looks in [`company/brand/design.md`](../../../../company/brand/design.md).
- **Purpose:** a video is for feeling the before and the after, not for listing features.
- **Open on the problem, not the product.** The first three seconds decide whether people keep watching: start in a moment of pain or recognition, not on a logo.
- **One idea.** A launch video makes one point land. Three things to explain are three videos.
- **Write for the ear and the eye together.** The voiceover is what they hear, the screen what they see. The two reinforce each other and never repeat. Keep the voiceover short: people read faster than you talk.
- **End on the one thing,** and a single call to action.
