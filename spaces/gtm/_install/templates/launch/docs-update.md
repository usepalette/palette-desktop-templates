# Docs update

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team updates its docs, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

The update to the product's docs for what shipped. Every launch with a change people will see updates the docs, Tier 1 included.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** an existing page in the same part of the docs. Match its style, its depth and its formatting: in docs, consistency beats creativity. The product's file in [`company/products/`](../../../../company/products/README.md) links to the docs.
- **Purpose:** practical, not persuasive. Someone lands here to use the feature; get them there fast.
- **What to include:** what the feature does, in one concrete paragraph; when to use it, as a situation ("When your team needs to…", not "This feature enables…"); how to start, in numbered, specific steps; and what it needs first, if anything.
- **What to skip:** marketing language, every configuration option (cover the common case and link the rest), screenshots that will go out of date, and anything that only repeats the website.
- **Tone:** helpful and direct, like a good README.
- **Cross-references:** link to and from the related pages, and update the getting-started guide, the FAQ or the overview where this touches them.
