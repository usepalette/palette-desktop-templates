# Playbooks — Content

**How content is thought about here**: the shared reasoning, not step-by-step checklists. Load the playbook before writing, so the approach is the same no matter who, or which agent, is writing.

A playbook is *philosophy plus approach*; the mechanical "do X then Y" belongs in a [process](../processes/README.md). Add one when you notice you keep re-explaining "how we approach X", such as how the team runs its newsletter. Name it for its domain.

## Playbooks

| Playbook | Load it when |
|---|---|
| [Content](content.md) | Before starting, refining or planning any piece: what a piece is for, how we write, the length and cadence of each channel, and how a piece reaches people |
