# Processes — Accounts

**How things are done here**: step-by-step, specific, actionable. A process can stand alone or be referenced by a [playbook](../playbooks/README.md). The playbook says *why and how to think*; the process says *what to do, in which order*.

A process written this way can be handed to an agent as it stands: name the process, and the steps are followed in order. That is the progression: first a person reads it, then an agent runs it.

## Processes

| Process | Run it when |
|---|---|
| [`prepare-a-call.md`](prepare-a-call.md) | Before a call with a customer or a lead: "prepare me for my call with [customer]" |
| [`read-account-health.md`](read-account-health.md) | Someone asks how an account is doing, or a renewal or a hard call is coming: "how is [customer] doing?" |
| [`review-accounts.md`](review-accounts.md) | Someone asks where the accounts stand, who has gone quiet or what is past due: "where do our accounts stand?" |

Adding an account and logging a call are the workspace's own steps, not processes here: say "we just signed a new customer" ([`customers/README.md`](../../../customers/README.md) → Add a customer) or "here are my notes from the call" ([`company/meetings.md`](../../../company/meetings.md)).
