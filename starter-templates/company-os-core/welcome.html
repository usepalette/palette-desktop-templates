Welcome to your workspace

A workspace for AI-native teams to work together. You, your colleagues and your agents all work from the same folder.

Why work this way

Any agentClaude Code, Codex, Gemini, Mistral or another one. They all read the same workspace, so everyone uses the one they like.
Context you ownWhat your company knows lives in plain files in your own folder, not locked inside one agent.
It builds upEvery session leaves the workspace a little smarter, for everyone's next one.

Basic concepts

1SessionYour own copy of the workspace to work in. Nothing you change there reaches your team until you save it.
→
2Your agentAsk for what you need in plain words. It starts from what is already in the workspace, so you explain less each time.
→
3SaveLook through what changed, then save it to the workspace. Now your colleagues and their agents have it too.

What you save is there for everyone's next session.

What is in this workspace

Your workspace
  • companyWho you are: the company on one page, how you work, your tools and your glossary
    • brandHow you sound and how you look
    • productsOne page per product or service
    • marketWho you sell to, and who you compete with
    • templatesFormats everyone reuses: a one-pager, a social post, a customer story
  • peopleOne folder per person: who they are and how to work with them. Yours is made when you get set up
  • spacesOne folder per area of work, like go-to-market, product or design
  • customersOne folder per customer or client: the brief and the meeting notes
  • projectsOne folder per project: the brief, the milestones, the decisions and the meetings
  • README.mdThe map: where everything is, and where a new thing goes
  • welcome.htmlThis page
  • AGENTS.mdThe rules for every agent that works here
  • lessons.mdWhat to do differently next time
  • log.mdWhat happened, and what was decided

Plus a few files that are only for your agents.
