# Customer blurb

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team writes to its customers, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

A short, personal message about what shipped, ready to send by Slack or email to one customer who would care. Tier 2 and 3.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the customer's brief and their last meeting notes in [`customers/`](../../../../customers/README.md), for what they asked for and the words they used; the agreed messaging brief; and the sender's `people/<name>/tone-of-voice.md`.
- **What it is:** a note to one person, or one customer, usually a champion who asked for it or has the problem it solves. Not a broadcast.
- **Make it about them, not us.** Lead with what they will care about, ideally something they raised. Refer to their own words or an earlier conversation only when the brief or a meeting note holds it. Never make one up.
- **Length:** three to five sentences, with one clear ask: try it, get on a call, share feedback.
- **Tone:** how the sender actually writes to them: warm, direct, no marketing polish. Match the relationship.
