# Competitor watch

What the competitors did this week and whether it matters, with a profile per competitor and where we stand.

## What it makes

One page in three views:

- **This week**: three numbers, the moves rated Matters, Watch or Note, what to say when a customer brings one up, and the earlier weeks.
- **Where we stand**: one table, us against each competitor.
- **Profiles**: one per competitor, with the price and the last move. Each profile opens to who they sell to, where they win, where we win and their recent moves.

Every Friday the new week goes on top, and the week before moves into **Earlier weeks**. The digest goes to marketing and sales, what matters first and what to say last, always after a preview.

## The request

> Watch our four competitors' changelogs, pricing pages and posts, pull the mentions from Slack and the calls, and write the weekly digest with what matters.

Said in the person's own words.

## What it reads

- **Web**: the competitors' changelogs, pricing pages, blogs and posts.
- **Slack**: the competitors channel and the mentions elsewhere.
- **Transcripts**: the sales calls where a competitor came up.
- **Notion or Google Docs**: the competitors page, for the profiles.
- **A space with a `competitors/` folder**: the profiles, when the workspace has one. The page reads them and never changes them.

Every source is optional. Without the calls, the sales mentions are empty and say so. Without a space or a competitors page, the profiles are made from the web and kept on the page.

## What lands in the workspace

In the folder the person picks, for example `spaces/marketing/`:

```
competitor-watch.html             the page
processes/competitor-watch.md     how the page is written, refreshed and posted
```

The process gets a row in `processes/README.md`.

## How it stays current

Ask for this week's competitor watch, or press Refresh on the page. Both run `processes/competitor-watch.md` on the page. Only the parts the template writes change. Lines the team changed stay as they are.
