## How we think about it

Content is useful first and promotional second. If someone reads a piece and learns something, even if they never buy, it did its job. The best content comes from real work: problems we have solved, decisions we have made, things we have learned.

- **Write from experience, not research alone.**
- **One strong idea beats five mediocre ones.**
- **Distribution matters as much as writing.** A great piece nobody sees is a wasted piece.
- **Consistency beats intensity.** Regular pieces over sporadic bursts.
- **Every piece has one clear takeaway.** The reader can say what the point was afterwards.

## Principles

- **Lead with the problem, then the relief.** Start with what hurts in real life, then show how it gets better.
- **Be concrete.** Use examples, outcomes and situations people recognise.
- **Respect scepticism.** Assume the reader has seen hype before. Don't oversell; explain what is real.
- **Avoid feature soup.** Talk in workflows and outcomes, not a list of capabilities.
- **Short on adjectives, strong on verbs.** Every sentence moves things forward. Cut filler.

## Approach

### Before writing

- Read the voice: [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../../company/brand/voice.md), and for a piece under a person's name, their `people/<name>/tone-of-voice.md` first.
- Check [`pieces/`](../pieces/README.md): has this been covered before?
- Name the one thing the reader should take away.
- Know where it will be published, and shape it for that channel (**Content types** below).

### While writing

- Lead with the insight or the problem, not the backstory.
- Use concrete examples: specific beats abstract.
- Keep paragraphs short, one idea each.
- Read it out loud. If you stumble, rewrite.
- Cut a fifth after the first draft. Then cut a tenth more.

### After writing

- Does it pass the [voice check](../processes/check-the-voice.md)?
- Would a sceptic find it credible?
- Is the takeaway clear in the first three sentences?

## Distribution

Publishing is half the job. For every piece:

1. **Primary channel.** Where it lives: the blog, LinkedIn, the newsletter.
2. **Amplification.** Where it is shared: communities, groups, threads, partners.
3. **Repurposing.** What else it becomes: a blog post into three LinkedIn posts, a case study into a talk.

## What we don't do

- Write generic "thought leadership" with no original insight.
- Publish AI-generated content without heavy editing and a real human perspective.
- Chase SEO keywords without having something genuine to say.
- Write about topics we have no direct experience with.
