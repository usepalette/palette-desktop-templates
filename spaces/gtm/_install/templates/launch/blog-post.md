# Blog post

The announcement post for a launch. Tier 2 and 3.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief, for the hook and the one-liner, and the pages under **Before using any format** in [`README.md`](README.md).
- **Length:** Tier 2, 300 to 500 words. Tier 3, 500 to 800. If it runs over, cut; don't summarise.
- **Opening:** never "We're excited to announce" or "Today we're launching". Open with the problem, or an observation the reader recognises. Create tension or recognition in the first two sentences.
- **Tone:** like explaining it to a smart colleague over coffee. Concrete, not abstract: "When your lead asks for a status update and you spend 20 minutes gathering it from three tools" beats "streamline your workflow".
- **Shape:** the problem, what we built, how it works, what changes. Feel the pain, see the solution, know what to do next. Don't meander.
- **Avoid:** feature soup (capabilities instead of outcomes), hedging ("helps you", "enables teams to"), and explaining what the reader already knows.
- **Before it goes out:** read it out loud. If you would roll your eyes hearing it, rewrite it. Then check that:
  - The opening creates curiosity or recognition, not hype
  - A sceptical reader would find it useful, not annoying
  - There is a concrete example or scenario
  - There is one call to action, and it is clear
  - It passes the self-check in [`playbooks/content.md`](../../playbooks/content.md) → Before it goes out
