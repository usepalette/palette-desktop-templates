# Market

Who the company sells to and what it is up against. Read before sales, outreach, positioning or a product decision that depends on who the customer is.

## What is here

| Path | Answers | Read it before |
|---|---|---|
| [`ideal-customer.md`](ideal-customer.md) | Who do we sell to, and who is not a fit? | outreach, qualifying a lead, writing for a buyer |
| [`competitors/`](competitors/README.md) | Who else could they choose, and how do we differ? | a competitive conversation, a comparison, positioning work |

## Adding to this folder

A market topic that neither page covers (a second segment, the size of the market, pricing research) gets its own file here and a row in the table above. Give it the same style as `ideal-customer.md`: a box at the top, and a bracket in every slot that says what goes there. A new competitor is a copy of the mold in [`competitors/`](competitors/README.md).
