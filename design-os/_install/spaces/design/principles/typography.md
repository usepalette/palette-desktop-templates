# Typography

> **Not confirmed yet.** A starter set. Change it until it says what the team believes, then delete this box.

How type is used. The typefaces, sizes and text styles are the design system's, in [`../design-system/foundations/`](../design-system/foundations/README.md).

## Text styles, never loose values

Every piece of text takes one of the design system's text styles. A style is chosen for its role, not for its size.

## Sentence case by default

Page titles, section headings, buttons, menus and labels are in sentence case. Title Case and ALL CAPS read louder than they look, and used everywhere they make the product feel formal. The names of features are proper names; see [`naming.md`](naming.md).

## Eyebrows sparingly

A small label above a heading adds noise more often than it helps. Use one only when the reader needs that context before the title, and never two on one screen.

## A minimum size for reading

Text meant to be read does not go below the size the team sets for it. Smaller sizes are for tight UI such as badges, never for meaning.
