# Research interview

> **Mold.** The format of the notes from one interview on a project; don't fill it in here. Copy it into the project as `projects/<name>/research/YYYY-MM-DD-<participant>.md`, named with the participant's first name or a pseudonym. A project without a research folder makes it first: [`README.md`](README.md) → Folders a project makes later.
>
> In the copy: everything from **Fill in** down, with its own full frontmatter as `type: research`, plus the interview's `date`, quoted ([`README.md`](README.md) → How templates work). Fill every section you have material for, and delete the lines you have nothing for rather than leave a bracket in real notes. Add a row to the research folder's `README.md`.

What one interview taught: who it was with, what the team wanted to learn, what they said, and what it changes for the project.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the project's goals, when it keeps them, so the interview ties back to an outcome.
- **No personal data.** A first name or a pseudonym, and no contact details. A recording stays where it was made: link it.
- **Quotes are exact.** Never tidy a quote into something the person did not say.
- **Follow-ups** go to the task home named in [`company/tools.md`](../../../company/tools.md); the note keeps the link.
- **Quality bar:** someone who was not there can tell what was learned and what it changes. When it changes the project's research findings, update them in the same session.
