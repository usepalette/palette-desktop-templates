# Messaging brief

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team briefs a launch, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

What a launch says, agreed before any piece is written. It is the output of Phase 1 in [`playbooks/launch.md`](../../playbooks/launch.md), and every piece in Phase 2 traces back to it.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the pages under **Before using any format** in [`README.md`](README.md); the customers' briefs and meeting notes in [`customers/`](../../../../customers/README.md), for who asked for it and in which words; the files in [`company/market/competitors/`](../../../../company/market/competitors/README.md), for how others frame the same thing.
- **Length and shape:** one page. Every section is filled in, or left open on purpose. The research tables hold only what a source says, with the source.
- **Quality bar:** the one-liner works in a changelog, a chat message and a post. The audience names roles, team sizes and situations, never "everyone". The hook is specific and creates curiosity or tension: "You're spending five hours a week on tasks a robot could do" beats "Introducing a better way to manage your workflow". The angle says how this is different, not only better.
- **Agreed before Phase 2.** Show it to the person and the team, and write no piece until it is agreed.
