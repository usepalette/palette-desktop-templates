# Brief

> **Mold.** The format of a project's brief; don't fill it in here. [`processes/start-a-project.md`](../processes/start-a-project.md) copies it into a project as `projects/<name>/brief.md`.
>
> In the copy: everything from **Fill in** down, with its own full frontmatter as `type: doc` ([`README.md`](README.md) → How templates work). Write only what you were given: a slot you have nothing for keeps its brackets, and a line you worked out yourself ends with "(to confirm)".

The single page a new joiner reads first: why the project exists, who it is for, what success looks like and what it has to work within. The project's `README.md` says what the project is in two lines; the brief says why, in one page.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the project's `README.md`, and the customer's brief in `customers/<name>/` when the project is for a customer.
- **Length and shape:** one page, every section in this order. Keep it tight and link out for the detail: to the project's goals, scope and timeline when it keeps them, rather than repeating them.
- **Quality bar:** someone who reads only this page can say what the project is for and how its success will be judged. Every claim comes from a person or a document.
