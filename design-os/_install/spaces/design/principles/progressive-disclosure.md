# Progressive disclosure

> **Not confirmed yet.** A starter set. Change it until it says what the team believes, then delete this box.

Every screen shows the least information that lets a person decide whether to go deeper. A screen that tries to answer every question at once answers none of them well.

## The test

For every element, ask whether it helps the person decide what to do next: open, pick or skip. If the information only becomes useful after that decision, it belongs one level deeper.

## Lists

A row carries what it is, plus the one or two facts that separate it from its neighbours. When a row needs more, move information to the detail view.

A status that is the same on nine of ten rows tells the person nothing. Show the exception, not the norm.

## Detail views

A detail view opens with the handful of facts that orient the person, then sections in the order they are used. The structure reads from the headings alone. History, records and rarely used sections stay collapsed or one level deeper.

## Edit views

An edit view shows the fields people actually change. Everything that can be derived is prefilled. Everything rare appears when asked for.

## The limit

The primary information and the primary action never hide behind a step. Hiding everything is not minimal, it is obscure. Decide-level information sits on the screen, act-level information one level down.
