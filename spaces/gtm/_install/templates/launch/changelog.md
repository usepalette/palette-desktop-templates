# Changelog

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team writes its changelog, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

The factual record of what shipped. Every launch gets an entry, whatever its tier.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief, for the one-liner. The voice still applies, but this is the one place you don't sell.
- **Purpose:** clarity, not storytelling or persuasion. Someone scanning it understands what changed in under ten seconds.
- **Length by tier:** Tier 1, one to three sentences, just the facts. Tier 2, a short paragraph: what changed, why, and what it makes possible. Tier 3, a paragraph and, when it helps, a few bullets with the main capabilities. Still short.
- **Lead with what changed, not why.** "You can now filter by team" comes before "We heard from customers that…".
- **Concrete language:** "You can now…" and "[Feature] now includes…", never "We're introducing…", "We're excited to share…" or "A new way to…".
- **Links and visuals:** link the docs and the blog post when they exist. Add a screenshot or a short recording for Tier 2 and 3 when it helps.
- **Tone:** neutral and clear. Say what happened.
