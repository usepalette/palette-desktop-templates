# Landing page

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team builds a landing page, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

The content of a page for one launch: what a visitor who arrived cold needs to understand it and want it. Tier 3 only.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief; [`company/brand/messaging.md`](../../../../company/brand/messaging.md), for the positioning and the proof points; the voice in [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../../../company/brand/voice.md); and [`company/brand/design.md`](../../../../company/brand/design.md) for the page itself.
- **Purpose:** the messaging brief, expanded and put in order for someone who arrived cold.
- **Order it like an argument:** the hook, the problem, the shift, what it is, how it works, the proof, the call to action. Each section earns the scroll to the next.
- **Rules:** one main call to action, repeated, never competing with another. Concrete scenarios over adjectives. Every claim backed by something real: a proof point in the messaging, or the product's file. Scannable: the headings and the subheadings carry the story on their own.
