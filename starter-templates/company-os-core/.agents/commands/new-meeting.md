# New meeting

Turn notes, a transcript or a spoken recap into one meeting note in the right folder, and update what the meeting changed.

Say "log this meeting" or "here are my notes from the call", or name the command.

1. Work from whatever the person has. The date, the people and the kind of meeting come from the material; ask only for what is missing.
2. Follow [`company/meetings.md`](../../company/meetings.md): it says which `meetings/` folder the note goes in and what else to update.
3. Say where the note is and what you updated.

## It adapts

- **Use what you were given.** Anything the person wrote after the command, or earlier in the chat, counts. Ask only for what is still missing, in one round.
- **The steps live in one place.** Follow the page named above. If that page says something different from this one, the page wins.
- **If the page named above is missing, stop.** It was renamed or removed. Look in the map in the root `README.md` for where meeting notes live now and use that. If there is no place for them, say so and ask whether to set one up. Never rebuild a missing folder or its mold from memory.
