# Brand

How the company presents itself: how it sounds, what it says and how it looks. Read before producing anything a customer, a prospect or the public will see.

## Files

| File | Answers | Read it before |
|---|---|---|
| [`voice.md`](voice.md) | How do we sound? | writing anything external |
| [`messaging.md`](messaging.md) | What do we say? | describing the company: a bio, a page, a pitch, a post |
| [`design.md`](design.md) | How do we look? | making anything visual: a page, a deck, a document, a chart |

## How the three fit together

Messaging is *what* is said, voice is *how* it is said, design is *how it looks*. A piece of writing loads the first two; anything with a layout loads all three.

When something goes out under a person's name, that person's own `tone-of-voice.md` (in their folder under [`people/`](../../people/README.md)) comes first, and the company voice sits under it.

## Adding to this folder

A brand topic that outgrows its page, or that none of the three covers (naming, photography, the story behind the company), gets its own file here and a row in the table above. Give it the same style as the three pages: a box at the top, and a bracket in every slot that says what goes there.

## When to update

Update these files when something about the brand actually changes: new positioning, a new tagline, a word the company stops using, a visual refresh. Change the file, then add a line to the company [`log.md`](../log.md) so the change has a date.

A correction to one draft ("too formal for this customer") is not a brand change. It goes in the author's `tone-of-voice.md` or the relevant `lessons.md`.
