# Launch templates

The formats for launch content, used with the workflow in [`playbooks/launch.md`](../../playbooks/launch.md). The playbook says which pieces a launch needs; each format says how to make one.

## Before using any format

Load these first. They set the tone, the words and the positioning of everything you make.

| Page | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../../../company/brand/voice.md) | How we sound, and the words we use and avoid |
| [`company/brand/messaging.md`](../../../../company/brand/messaging.md) | The positioning, the one-liner, the tagline and the proof points |
| The launch's messaging brief | What this launch says. Every piece traces back to it |

Add [`company/brand/design.md`](../../../../company/brand/design.md) for anything visual, and the author's `people/<name>/tone-of-voice.md` for anything under a person's name.

**The words matter.** The words to always use and the words to never use are in the voice page, under **Words**. They carry the positioning: get them wrong and you fight your own story. They apply to all external content, not only blog posts.

## Rules for every format

They apply to every piece, so the formats don't repeat them.

- **One call to action per piece.** One clear action, not three.
- **No hype.** No "excited to announce", "game-changing" or "revolutionary".
- **The problem first, then the relief.** In every format.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Real situations, roles and tools. "Your team lead asks for a status update" beats "streamline alignment".
- **Trace it to the messaging brief.** Every piece connects back to the agreed brief. If you can't connect it, you are drifting.

## How to use them

1. Follow the workflow in [`playbooks/launch.md`](../../playbooks/launch.md).
2. Start with the messaging brief: fill it in during Phase 1 and get it agreed.
3. Once the messaging is agreed, pick the formats by tier (Phase 2).
4. Copy the part under **Fill in** to where the piece is written ([`playbooks/launch.md`](../../playbooks/launch.md) → Where the work lives), and fill it in there. The formats here keep their brackets.
