# Playbooks — GTM

**How a domain is thought about here**: the shared reasoning, not step-by-step checklists. Load the relevant playbook before doing that kind of work, so the approach is consistent no matter who, or which agent, is doing it.

A playbook is *philosophy plus approach*; the mechanical "do X then Y" belongs in a [process](../processes/README.md), and the format to fill in in a [template](../templates/README.md). A playbook earns its place once there is a real point of view on the domain. Add one when you notice you keep re-explaining "how we approach X". Name it for its domain.

## Playbooks

| Playbook | Load it when |
|---|---|
| [`sales.md`](sales.md) | Preparing for a sales call, running one or following it up, or judging whether a lead is a fit |
| [`content.md`](content.md) | Writing anything the company will publish: a post, an article, a newsletter, a case study |
| [`launch.md`](launch.md) | Shipping a product update to the world, from a changelog entry to a full launch |

The three ship as a starter point of view, each marked "Not confirmed yet". Read them as a team, change what you disagree with, delete what doesn't apply, and delete the box on a playbook once it says how you work.

Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) when a new one is written, named for its domain, such as pricing or events, and list it here.

## When another space keeps the same kind of playbook

An Accounts space keeps a sales playbook of its own, and a Content space a content playbook. When the workspace has one of them as well as this space, one page is the home and the other space links to it, so the team keeps one. Which page is the home is asked when the second space is installed, and a copy is removed only after a yes.
