# Community post

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team posts in its community, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

A short post about what shipped, in the community channel, for people who already use the product. Tier 1 and up, and for release notes between launches.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief, and the voice in [`company/brand/voice.md`](../../../../company/brand/voice.md). The same plain voice as everything else, only lighter.
- **What it is:** a post in the community channel, on Slack or Discord. The readers already use the product and are friendly. Don't sell: tell them what shipped and invite a reaction.
- **Tone:** native to the channel, like writing in a team channel rather than a marketing post. Short paragraphs, line breaks for scanning, casual but not goofy.
- **Length:** about 80 to 150 words. If it doesn't fit in a single message preview, it is too long.
- **The shape that works:** a one-line headline saying what shipped, in their words; three to five bullets with the most useful parts, not all of them; a link to the full changelog or the download; one sentence inviting reactions or questions.
- **Avoid:** formal greetings, email sign-offs, the changelog's words copied as they are, "we're excited to share", "big news", and engagement bait.
- **Emoji:** sparingly. One in the headline if it earns its place, and perhaps one warm one at the end.
