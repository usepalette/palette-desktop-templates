# Company OS Core

A workspace for the whole company: who you are, your people, your areas of work, your customers and your projects, in one folder of markdown.

People and agents read the same files, so what one person settles is there for the next one.

## What you get

- **The company on one page**, with how you work, your tools, a glossary, your brand, your products and your market, each in its own small file.
- **A folder per person**, with how to work with them and how they write.
- **Spaces** for each area of work, such as go-to-market, product or design. Each space has its own front door, rules, lessons and log.
- **A folder per customer and per project**, with the brief, the decisions and the meeting notes.
- **Eleven commands** for the work people ask for again and again: add a person, start a project, log a meeting, add a space, check the workspace and more.
- **Lessons and a log** at every level, so the workspace remembers what was learned and what was decided.

## What lands in the workspace

The whole folder below becomes the new workspace.

```
company/      who you are: the company, brand, products, market, shared formats
people/       one folder per person
spaces/       one folder per area of work
customers/    one folder per customer, client or account
projects/     one folder per project
README.md     the map of the workspace
welcome.html  the page anyone new opens first
AGENTS.md     how agents work here
SETUP.md      present only while setup is in progress
.agents/      file conventions and one page per command
lessons.md    what to do differently next time
log.md        what happened, and what was decided
```

## How setup works

Nothing runs when the workspace is made. On Home, **Set up** starts a session that follows `SETUP.md`:

1. It makes your own profile in `people/`.
2. It drafts the company pages from the fastest source you have, such as a website, a deck or a document.
3. It shows the drafts and writes them after your yes.
4. It ends with your first space.

Anyone can continue setup later, in any order. `SETUP.md` is deleted when setup is done.

## What builds on it

Spaces install into `spaces/`, and artifacts install into any folder of the workspace. Both use the structure and the conventions this starter sets.
