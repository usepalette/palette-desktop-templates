# Social post

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the company writes its posts, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the company has made the format its own.

A post for a social channel, under a person's name or the company's.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** when it goes out under a person's name, that person's `tone-of-voice.md` in their folder under [`people/`](../../people/README.md); their voice comes before the company's. For a company post, [`../brand/voice.md`](../brand/voice.md). Use [`../brand/messaging.md`](../brand/messaging.md) for facts and names only, never to paste a tagline into a post.
- **One idea per post.** Pick the angle before writing: something built, a problem seen up close, a moment with a customer, an opinion, something learned.
- **Length and shape:** short. The first line has to earn the second. No list of features.
- **Quality bar:** the author would post it as it stands. It says only what happened: never a reaction, a result or a conversation nobody told you about.
