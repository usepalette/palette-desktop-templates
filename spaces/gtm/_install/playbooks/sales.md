# Sales

> **Not confirmed yet.** A starter playbook. Change it until it says how the team sells, then delete this box.

How we think about selling: discovery before the demo, honesty about fit, and a clear next step. Load it before a sales call, a follow-up, or a decision on whether a lead is worth pursuing.

## How we think about it

We understand the customer's world before we talk about ours. Discovery first, demo second. If we can't describe their problem better than they can, we haven't earned the right to pitch.

## Principles

- **The best sales conversations feel like consulting, not pitching.**
- **Customers buy when they feel understood, not when they feel impressed.**
- **Specifics beat superlatives.** "Saves three hours a week" beats "revolutionary".
- **If the product isn't right for them, say so early.** Trust compounds.

## Approach

### Before the call

- Read what we have on the customer: their brief and the last two meeting notes, in their folder in [`customers/`](../../../customers/README.md)
- Check their website, their LinkedIn and recent news
- Have a hypothesis about their problem, and hold it loosely. [`company/market/ideal-customer.md`](../../../company/market/ideal-customer.md) says what usually starts the search
- Prepare three to five discovery questions, not a slide deck. The story and its questions are in [`foundations/sales-narrative.md`](../foundations/sales-narrative.md)

### During the call

**Discovery, 60 to 70% of the time**

- Start with their world, not ours
- Ask open questions about how they work today
- Listen for pain, and write it down in their words
- Find out who else is involved in the decision

**Positioning, 20 to 30%**

- Connect their pain to what we do
- Use their language, not our marketing language
- Show, don't tell: demo the workflow that answers their pain
- Be honest about what is live and what is coming. The product's file in [`company/products/`](../../../company/products/README.md) says which

**Next steps, 5 to 10%**

- Always end with a clear next step
- Propose a specific action, not "let's stay in touch"

### After the call

- Log the meeting: say "log this meeting", and the note goes in the customer's folder with their brief brought up to date. A customer without a folder gets one once there is real engagement ("we just signed a new customer")
- Put the follow-ups in the task home named in [`company/tools.md`](../../../company/tools.md)
- Send a follow-up within 24 hours

## What we don't do

- Pitch cold, without research
- Lead with a demo before we understand the problem
- Promise timelines we can't keep
- Discount to close. If price is the only objection, it isn't the right fit
- Use pressure tactics, such as "limited-time offer" or "other companies are signing up"

## Qualification

Who we sell to, and who is not a fit, is in [`company/market/ideal-customer.md`](../../../company/market/ideal-customer.md). In a conversation, listen for these.

### Good signals

- They describe a real pain, not a theoretical interest
- They have tried to solve it before, which shows urgency
- The person on the call can influence the decision
- They ask detailed questions about how it works

### Red flags

- "Just exploring", with no specific pain
- No budget authority, and no path to it
- They want a feature we don't have and won't build
- The timeline is "someday"
