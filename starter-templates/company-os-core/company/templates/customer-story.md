# Customer story

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the company tells customer stories, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the company has made the format its own.

What changed for a customer, in their words where possible. Used on the website, in sales conversations and in proposals.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the customer's folder under [`customers/`](../../customers/README.md): the brief and the meeting notes are the source. Then [`../brand/voice.md`](../brand/voice.md) for how it sounds, and [`../brand/messaging.md`](../brand/messaging.md) for how we describe ourselves.
- **Approval comes first.** Nothing about a customer is published without their yes. Record who approved, what, for which channels and when, at the bottom of the story.
- **Length and shape:** as long as it needs and no longer. The situation, what they did, what changed. The customer is the main character, not us.
- **Quality bar:** every number and every quote can be traced to a meeting note or a message. Quotes are exact; never tidy a quote into something they didn't say.
