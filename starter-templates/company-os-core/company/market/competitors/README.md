## Add a competitor

1. **Check it doesn't exist.** One competitor, one file.
2. **Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md)** to a file named for the company, in kebab-case.
3. **Fill it from sources you can cite:** their website, their pricing page, public reviews, what customers and lost deals told us. Use their own words for how they describe themselves. A line you worked out yourself ends with "(to confirm)", and a slot you have nothing for keeps its bracket.
4. **Stamp the frontmatter**, including `resource` (their website) and `review_by` (when to check again), and replace the mold box with **Not confirmed yet** until the sourced draft is reviewed, alone or in a named batch. Follow [page states](../../../.agents/page-states.md).
5. **List it** in the table above, replacing the "None yet" row if it is the first.

## Rules

- **Fair and sourced.** Strengths are written as honestly as weaknesses. Every claim about a competitor has a source and a date.
- **Facts go stale.** Pricing, features and funding change. Check the date before quoting a file, and update it when you learn something new.
