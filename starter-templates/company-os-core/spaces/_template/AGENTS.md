## Rules

## Updating this space

- Learned something durable about the domain → `foundations/`.
- Found a better way of thinking about a domain → the playbook. A better way of doing something → the process.
- A correction about working here → `lessons.md`. A decision or a change → `log.md`.
- A rule the person states for working here → this file, under **Rules**. What is here and where things go → `README.md`.
