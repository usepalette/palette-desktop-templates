# Products

One file per product or service the company sells: what it is, who it is for and how we talk about it. Read one file, know the product. These files are the source that pages, decks, proposals and posts get written from.

If you sell services, packages or programmes, use your own word in the heading and on this page. The folder itself stays `products/`, because links across the workspace point at it.

## Add a product

1. **Check it doesn't exist.** One product, one file.
2. **Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md)** to a file named for the product, in kebab-case.
3. **Write only what you were given.** Use the company's own words from its pages, decks and proposals. A line you worked out yourself ends with "(to confirm)", and a slot you have nothing for keeps its bracket. Never expand one line into a paragraph, and never invent a benefit, a customer or a number.
4. **Stamp the frontmatter** (`owner`, `stage`, `last_updated`, `last_updated_by`) and replace the mold box with **Not confirmed yet** for drafted content. Follow [page states](../../.agents/page-states.md): a reviewed page or named batch becomes `active`; directly supplied facts need no second approval.
5. **List it** in the table above, replacing the "None yet" row if it is the first.

## What belongs here, and what doesn't

- **Here:** how we describe and sell the product. The why, the what, who it is for, and a short how.
- **Not here:** how to use it step by step (that is the documentation, linked from the product file), and what is being built next (that is the tracker, or a product space under [`spaces/`](../../spaces/README.md)).
- **Later:** when the company has them, a product file can grow sections for a fuller description, what makes it different, objections and answers, and what is included. Add them when there is something real to put in them.
