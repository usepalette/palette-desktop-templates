# Writing in the product

> **Not confirmed yet.** The first section is a starter set and the second is not filled in. Change what doesn't fit, fill in your words, and delete this box when it describes how you write.

The words on a screen are part of the design, written with it and never added at the end. How the brand sounds in marketing is a different register, kept with the brand.

## How we write

- **Sentence case** for every label, button and heading.
- **Say what the person gets,** not how the system works.
- **Buttons say the action:** "Save changes", not "OK".
- **Errors say what happened and what to do next,** without blame.
- **Empty states say what will be here and how to start.**
- **Plain words over internal ones.** A word the team made up for itself does not reach the screen until a person would use it too.
- **Titles say what the thing is.** A title is a label, not an argument.
- **The same thing has the same name everywhere.** The words we use are listed below.
