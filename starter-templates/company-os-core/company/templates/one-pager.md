# One-pager

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the company makes one-pagers, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the company has made the format its own.

The company or one product on a single page: something to send after a first conversation, or to leave behind after a meeting.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** [`../brand/messaging.md`](../brand/messaging.md) for the lines, the product's file in [`../products/`](../products/README.md) when it is about one product, [`../brand/voice.md`](../brand/voice.md) for how it sounds, and [`../brand/design.md`](../brand/design.md) when it is laid out as a page or a PDF.
- **Write for one reader.** Name who it is for before writing. [`../market/ideal-customer.md`](../market/ideal-customer.md) says what that reader cares about.
- **Length and shape:** one page, read in under two minutes. The reader's problem first, then what we do, then proof, then one next step.
- **Quality bar:** every line is specific to this company and this reader. No claim without a proof point from the messaging or the product's file. Nothing invented.
