# Process

How design work is done, from understanding a problem to handing a design to engineering. The principles that judge the result are in [`../principles/`](../principles/README.md), and the settled calls in [`../rules/`](../rules/README.md).

| You need to… | Read |
|---|---|
| Explore a design from the problem up: a new product, a new section, or a screen that needs rethinking | [`exploration.md`](exploration.md), from the data model to the pitch |
| Break a design down into a scope that engineering builds from | [`scope-documents.md`](scope-documents.md) |
| Review a design against our guidance, and write back what it settles | [`reviews.md`](reviews.md) |

These pages are starters. Change them when the team works differently, and log the change in the space's `log.md`.
