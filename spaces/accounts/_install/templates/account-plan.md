# Account plan

> **Mold.** Copy the part under **Fill in** into a customer's brief, `customers/<name>/README.md`, as its **Plan** section, right after **What they care about**; don't fill it in here.
>
> In the copy: write what the person told you, in their words. End a line you worked out yourself with "(to confirm)", and leave a slot you have nothing for in its brackets. An objective alone is a fine start: the bet and the risks keep their brackets until there is something real to put there. The section takes no frontmatter of its own: stamp the brief's `last_updated` and `last_updated_by`, and follow `.agents/page-states.md` at the workspace root for a drafted section.

What the team is trying to achieve with an account, what it is doing about it now, and what could get in the way. It sits in the customer's brief, so a call brief and a review read it with everything else about the account.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the brief and its last two meeting notes, its **Health** section when it has one, and [`../playbooks/sales-approach.md`](../playbooks/sales-approach.md).
- **Length and shape:** three short parts, two or three sentences each at most. A plan that needs more is probably a project: work with a start and an end goes in [`projects/`](../../../projects/README.md), linked from the brief's **Projects**.
- **Quality bar:** the objective says what we want to be true; the bet is something the team is doing now, not a wish; each risk is something that could really block it.
- **When it changes:** rewrite the section, and add a dated `Decision:` line to the brief's **Activity** when the objective or the bet changes.
