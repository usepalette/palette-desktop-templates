# Account messaging

> **Starter format.** Usable as it stands. Change it to how the team matches a launch to its customers, or delete it and its row in `README.md`. The brackets under "Fill in" are the format: they stay. This box goes once the team has made the format its own.

Which customers a launch matters to, and the angle for each: the bridge from a launch to the customers who would care. Tier 2 and 3.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the agreed messaging brief, and the customers' briefs and meeting notes in [`customers/`](../../../../customers/README.md).
- **What it is:** instead of sending everyone the same message, match the launch to the customers it actually matters to, and give each one its own angle.
- **How to build it:** look through the briefs and the meeting notes for who asked for this or has the problem it solves. For each, note the champion, why it matters to them, and the angle in one line. Turn the strongest matches into a message each, from [`customer-blurb.md`](customer-blurb.md).
- **Be honest about fit.** A weak match with a generic message is worse than not reaching out. List a customer only when there is a real reason, with where it comes from.
