# Research findings

> **Mold.** The format of a project's running research synthesis; don't fill it in here. Copy it into the project once, as `projects/<name>/research/findings.md`, when there is research to draw together, and keep that copy current. A project without a research folder makes it first: [`README.md`](README.md) → Folders a project makes later.
>
> In the copy: everything from **Fill in** down, with its own full frontmatter as `type: research` ([`README.md`](README.md) → How templates work). Write only what the research says: a section you have nothing for keeps its brackets until it does.

A living synthesis of all the research on a project: what keeps coming up, what surprised the team, what is still open, and where each finding comes from. Updated as each interview or study lands.

## How to write this

- **Load first:** the interviews and studies in the project's research folder, and the project's goals when it keeps them.
- **Every theme names its sources:** how many people raised it, and in which notes. A theme from one person says so.
- **Quotes are exact,** and each comes from a note in the folder.
- **Quality bar:** someone who read none of the interviews knows what the research found, how sure it is, and what it changes for the project.
