# Companies

One profile per company that product feedback comes from. A profile holds only the context needed to read that company's signal, and links to every entry from it.

## Add or update a profile

- Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) here as `<company>.md`, named in lowercase kebab-case.
- Update the profile whenever a piece of feedback, an interview, a request or a painpoint from the company is added.
- Never copy account or CRM detail here.

## Customers

A profile here is not a customer record. When the company is also a customer, with a folder in [`customers/`](../../../../customers/README.md) at the workspace root, its profile sets `customer` to that folder's name and links its brief. The brief stays the one place for who they are, their people and where the relationship stands; never copy from it.
